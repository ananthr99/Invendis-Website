ABOUT US

We Are Silbo

Introducing SILBO, the product of Invendis Technologies, a trailblazer in manufacturing networking, IoT, and edge computing products tailored to meet diverse industrial needs. SILBO products stand out for their flexibility in adapting to evolving technology and providing seamless deployment solutions, transcending geographical and environmental constraints. Our commitment extends to delivering advanced technologies, such as 5G-ready routers, gateways, and IoT products, equipped with multi-WAN functionality, industrial protocol support, VPN, cloud communication, and a planned integration of SD-WAN to meet market demands.
