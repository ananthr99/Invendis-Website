SILBO Networking Products

Industrial networking hardware for mission-critical industrial and telecom environments.

SILBO is Invendis's dedicated industrial networking brand — delivering rugged routers, gateways, protocol converters, and switches purpose-built for Industry 4.0 connectivity challenges.

Multi-WAN Router

4G/5G, Wi-Fi 4/5/6, and dual-SIM with load balancing and VPN support for secure industrial connectivity.

Industrial Gateway

Edge computing gateways for data aggregation, local processing, and cloud connectivity at industrial sites.

Protocol Converter

Modbus, DNP3, IEC, and SNMP protocol bridging for legacy equipment integration and industrial automation.

Network Switch
